Interactive simulations and written studies from my projects. Each one is a single self-contained page.

Simulations

  • HNSW Explorer. Build and search a hierarchical navigable small world graph one step at a time with the running code beside it. An LSH entry point mode shows where seeding the search from a hash bucket changes the code and the search.
  • SKOPE door-event simulator. Play one fridge door event, a pack removed or inserted, and pick one of six camera layouts and watch each camera's ray-cast view. Each camera reports how much of the moving pack it sees. Options cover tilted gravity-feed shelves, a dairy pack mix and random stock.
  • Deep Nested LSH Explorer. Build a nested tree of AND and OR levels over MinHash rows and watch its candidate probability curve, error areas and tails update live. Presets from the study compare standard banding with deeper trees at the same row budget.

Studies

  • Subagent crew study. An independent look at how well a crew of AI helpers works for software development, with one planning the change, one writing the code and one reviewing it.
