Kalah TDD Experiment

Classic greedy TDD writes the smallest failing test and then the smallest change that passes it. Do TDD variants that plan the class structure first, change the step size, try several starts or change how the tests check the code lead an AI coding agent to a better Kalah solution?

Run ended 2026-10-16. Model Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5). Runs: full-1 survey, tcp-1-list2 list reliability, rep-1 planned contrasts, rep-2 planned contrasts. 11 survey methods. Claims carry a tag. Recomputed means redone from source data. Verified means checked against a primary source. Unchecked means written or judged by an agent and not checked.

The study has a survey and two contrast runs. A survey ran 11 methods once each in run full-1. Every build reached the functional ceiling of 15/15. In rep-2 no survey leader differed from Classic greedy TDD on M, and Held-out TDD scored a lower adjusted mutation score with Holm p 0.024. Tag: recomputed.

Q1 How far does the analogy of classic TDD with greedy pathing or gradient descent hold, and do variants that change the map, the step size, the breadth of search or the check reach a better end point?

Classic TDD writes the smallest failing test and then makes the smallest change that passes it, which resembles greedy pathing or gradient descent toward the requirements.

The analogy holds for the climb and no variant reached a better end point. All 10 variants and Classic greedy TDD reached the same functional end point of 15/15 with 76/76 hidden and 69/69 unseen tests. No LOG.md row of the 14 full-1 clones lowered the hidden pass count, so no method stepped back.

The methods differed in how early tests passed per weighted token. At 500000 weighted tokens held-out passed 0.42 of the hidden tests, coarse-to-fine and mutation-guided 0.39, classic 0.29, annealed 0.26 and tpp 0.05. Noun-verb, functions-first, mapped-path, property-based and multi-start passed none.

At 750000 noun-verb, functions-first and mutation-guided passed all 76 tests and classic and held-out 0.68. Mapped-path, property-based and multi-start passed 0.03 or less. In rep-2, a different batch from the survey, the median share passed at 500000 weighted tokens over 5 replicates was 0.00 for held-out, 0.34 for coarse-to-fine, 0.09 for mutation-guided and 0.29 for classic, a descriptive figure with no test.

Multi-start's 3 start clones alone cost 869732 weighted tokens before its final clone began. Later commits deleted 33 of the 147 src lines classic added, a rewrite share of 22 percent. Functions-first rewrote 43 percent, annealed 35 and tpp 34.

The other 7 methods rewrote 11 percent or less, with none for held-out and multi-start. So the small steps of classic did not lead to the largest rewrite. Each survey method ran once on one task, so the survey figures are descriptive and a gap among them may be run noise.

Q2 Can each TDD methodology be written as a short numbered procedure that an AI coding agent carries out unattended from start to finish?

Yes for all 11.

Each procedure has 7 or 8 steps and every method reached a final clone with no failed collection step. The harness turned minute timeboxes into cycle caps and throwaway branches into uncommitted trials. Rule-only steps got no agent of their own.

Two calls hit the 15-cycle cap.

Glossary

The terms and short labels this page uses.

Glossary, 35 rows and 3 columns

TDD (test-driven development). Tag: verified.

Definition: A development practice that writes each test before the code that passes it and lets the tests steer the design. Lecture 11.2 treats it as a design process and not a testing technique.

Method and slug. Tag: verified.

Definition: A method is one TDD methodology written as a short numbered procedure in runs/full-1/tdd-methodologies.md. Its slug is its short lowercase name, which the Branch column shows after method/.

Kalah. Tag: verified.

Definition: A board game for two players. Each player has a row of houses holding seeds and a store. A move sows the seeds of one house round the board one at a time. The A02 task is a Java console program that plays Kalah in four variants of board size and sowing direction.

Family. Tag: verified.

Definition: One of four groups of methods in the Summary table of runs/full-1/tdd-methodologies.md, by what each changes in classic TDD. Baseline holds Classic greedy TDD alone. Structure first maps the classes before or after the code is written. Step size changes the size or order of the steps or lets early refactors accept a worse design. Search and validation tries several starts or changes how the tests check the code. The figure legends colour and shape each mark by family.

Cycle. Tag: verified.

Definition: One red-green pass after Beck's steps in Lecture 11.2. Add a test, see it fail, make it pass, then refactor. One agent call runs at most 15 cycles.

Iteration and loop. Tag: verified.

Definition: A loop is a run of method steps that repeats. Each pass is one iteration. A loop runs at most 15 iterations in full-1 and 2 in the pilots.

Clone and start clone. Tag: verified.

Definition: A clone is a fresh copy of the starter repository where one method's agents work. Multi-start TDD builds 3 start clones before its final clone, so 11 methods use 14 clones.

Arm. Tag: verified.

Definition: One configuration of a run. In tcp-1 each arm follows one order of the same test list.

Hidden, seen, unseen and acceptance tests. Tag: verified.

Definition: Hidden tests are the 76 TestKalah tests of the A02 course test suite, kept from every agent and used for grading. Seen tests are the 7 hidden tests whose specs the starter shows, as harness/grade/seen.tsv lists them. Six come as example spec files in examples/specs and one as the STA 6-4 simple_start spec quoted in the starter README. Unseen tests are the other 69. Acceptance tests are the 6 seen tests with example spec files, so the README test counts as seen only.

Functional mark /15. Tag: verified.

Definition: The functionality mark of the A02 rubric computed from the hidden tests. The Columns section gives its formula.

STA and STC. Tag: verified.

Definition: Spec folder names for the sowing direction. STA means anticlockwise and STC clockwise sowing. Pairs such as 6-4 and 7-5 give houses and seeds.

A01 and A02. Tag: verified.

Definition: The first two assignments of the course. The A02 spec and rubric define the task here and the A02 reference row is a solution to it. The A01 rubric used the course PMD ruleset.

AI and AI coding agent. Tag: verified.

Definition: AI stands for artificial intelligence. An AI coding agent is a language model that edits code and runs commands unattended.

PMD and strict PMD compliance. Tag: verified.

Definition: PMD is a static analysis tool for Java. Strict PMD compliance passes when the course ruleset kalah_pmd.xml finds no violation of any priority in src/.

CPU. Tag: verified.

Definition: Central processing unit. Each bench run uses one processor core.

Mutation score and K/N. Tag: verified.

Definition: Mutation testing makes small changes to the code, called mutants, and counts those the tests catch. K/N is killed plus timed-out mutants out of the N that compiled.

Adjusted mutation score. Tag: verified.

Definition: The mutation score after dropping each surviving mutant that fails none of the hidden tests the unmutated clone passes. Such a mutant need not pass all 76 hidden tests. It is likely equivalent to the original code or a gap in the hidden suite.

Weighted tokens. Tag: verified.

Definition: Model tokens weighted by relative price. Input counts 1, cache creation 1.25, cache read 0.05 and output 5 per token. The weights state no dollar cost.

Alterability and change case. Tag: verified.

Definition: Alterability is the cost of a change compared with its expected effort and likelihood. A change case is one likely change with its likelihood and impact. docs/SPEC.md names 2 change cases. No change case was carried out and the rating leaves alterability out, so it is not measured.

Blind review and blind rating. Tag: verified.

Definition: A review agent scored each method's code and tests once from a prompt that left out the method's name, and the blind rating supersedes those scores. In the blind rating three raters score each item from 1 to 5 on each cqp.html principle. An item is a rebuilt clone whose history names no method. The finals are the 11 final clones. The calibration items add the A02 reference, the starter stub and 7 mutants of functions-first, each damaging one or two named principles.

Audit flag. Tag: verified.

Definition: A command or commit by an agent that may show access to the hidden tests. Each high flag gets a disposition.

Test list. Tag: verified.

Definition: The list of tests a TDD developer plans to write before writing them.

Test case prioritisation. Tag: verified.

Definition: Ordering a test suite so that the tests most likely to reveal faults run first, after Rothermel et al. 2001. tcp-1 carries its criteria over to ordering a TDD test list.

Kendall distance. Tag: verified.

Definition: The share of test pairs that two orders rank differently. It is 0 for the same order and 1 for the reverse.

APFD-I. Tag: verified.

Definition: The area under a clone's hidden-pass curve over its steps, normalised to 0 to 1. Higher means hidden tests start to pass earlier. harness/report/curve.py defines it.

APFDc-H. Tag: verified.

Definition: The area under the same hidden-pass curve over cumulative weighted tokens divided by the cost to completion. harness/report/curve.py defines it.

Cost to completion. Tag: verified.

Definition: Weighted tokens of the lead cost plus the final clone's step costs, measure and review agents left out. A method's steps are the steps.csv rows of its graded points. An arm's steps are its loop calls, each costed by the costs.json agent its batch.json tcp.calls label names. A stopped call without a sha counts though it grades no point. harness/report/curve.py defines it.

M and M_io. Tag: verified.

Definition: M is the mean weighted-token cost to pass a hidden test, the cost to completion times 1 minus APFDc-H. M_io is M with output tokens left out of every cost, so it compares across CLI builds whose output per request differs. harness/report/curve.py defines both.

Survey and survey build. Tag: verified.

Definition: The survey is run full-1, which built each of the 11 methods once. A survey build is that one build of a method.

Replicate and representative build. Tag: verified.

Definition: A replicate is one of the 5 builds of a method in run rep-1 or rep-2. The representative build is the middle replicate by adjusted mutation score, then by lower cost to completion.

Planned contrast. Tag: verified.

Definition: A pair of methods and an outcome fixed before any build of its run. rep-1 holds two, Functions first, classes later against Noun-verb map and Anchor Engineering against Classic greedy TDD, each on adjusted mutation score by an exact Mann-Whitney U test with Holm correction over the two. rep-2 holds three, Held-out TDD, Coarse-to-fine TDD and Most informative next test each against Classic greedy TDD, on M by the same test with Holm correction over the three.

Batch. Tag: verified.

Definition: The builds launched together in one run. full-1, rep-1 and rep-2 are three batches, and the costs of full-1 do not compare with those of rep-1 or rep-2. The Limits explain why.

CQP and CQP accepted. Tag: verified.

Definition: CQP sums a final's rating medians over the four counted cqp.html principles fixed before rep-1, Simple Constructs, Minimal Duplication, Modular Structure and Problem Alignment, from 4 to 20. CQP accepted sums the accepted ones, from 3 to 15. The CQP sensitivity check tests each planned contrast on CQP accepted.

Relative spread and consistency score C. Tag: verified.

Definition: A method's relative spread on one metric is the mean absolute deviation of its replicates from their median over the mean of that deviation across the replicated methods off the metric's bound. C is the mean of its relative spreads on unadjusted mutation score, classes and cost to completion. Lower means more consistent. A metric with at least 60 percent of a method's builds at its bound reads at bound and leaves C and the other methods' mean. A metric with fewer than two methods off its bound reads not comparable.

Awards and E. Tag: verified.

Definition: The Headline's awards rank the survey methods, lower first, with ties sharing a place. E sums a final's distinct PMD findings, adjusted surviving mutants, hidden failures and unseen failures. Alignment needs the blind CQP rating to agree with PMD on every principle and PMD to report no issue. Best overall is the plain sum of each method's ranks on M among 15/15 methods, E and alignment. Cheapest, the lowest cost to completion, and most consistent design, the lowest C pooled over every replicate of rep-1 and rep-2, take bronze badges only and never count toward best overall. A medal only styles a place and carries no score. harness/report/awards.py defines them.
