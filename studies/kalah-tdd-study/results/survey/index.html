Survey (full-1)

Each survey method ran once, so its figures describe one build. On the share of hidden tests passed at 500000 weighted tokens Held-out TDD led at 0.42, then Coarse-to-fine TDD and Most informative next test at 0.39, against 0.29 for Classic greedy TDD. Held-out TDD also had the lowest adjusted mutation score of the survey at 0.794. In rep-2, a different batch from the survey, the median shares at 500000 weighted tokens were 0.00 for Held-out TDD, 0.34 for Coarse-to-fine TDD, 0.09 for Most informative next test and 0.29 for Classic greedy TDD.
