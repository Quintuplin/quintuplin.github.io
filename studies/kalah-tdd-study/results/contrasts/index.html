Planned contrasts (rep-2)

Three contrasts were fixed before any rep-2 build, each survey leader against Classic greedy TDD on M by an exact Mann-Whitney U test with Holm correction over the three. Held-out TDD gave U 11 and p 0.841, Coarse-to-fine TDD U 9 and p 0.548 and Most informative next test U 17 and p 0.421, each with Holm p 1.000. The M_io sensitivity check gave p 0.690, 0.690 and 0.310 and Holm p 1.000, 1.000 and 0.929. The planned quality outcome fell back to adjusted mutation score because Simple Constructs reached alpha 0.52 on the rep-2 finals, below 0.7. Held-out TDD scored lower than Classic greedy TDD, a median of 0.777 against 1.000, with U 0, p 0.008 and Holm p 0.024. The other two leaders gave Holm p 1.000. The CQP sensitivity check gave Held-out TDD a median of 13 against 10, U 24, p 0.024 and Holm p 0.071, and the other two Holm p 0.175. CQP accepted sums the three principles accepted on the full-1 finals, and the rep-2 rating accepts none of them, as the Limits explain. Tag: recomputed.
