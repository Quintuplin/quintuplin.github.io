This page sets out how the study scores its awards, which test answers each question and what the study cannot show. It defines no metric. M, E, C and every other measure are defined on Methodology: measures, and short labels such as CQP and PMD in the Glossary. The medals are on the landing page. The test results are on the results pages for the survey, the planned contrasts and consistency.

Award scoring

Each award but best overall places the methods on one value, lower first. places gives competition places, ties sharing the best place. podium keeps the places up to TOP = 3 and award gives them the medals gold, silver and bronze. Best overall ranks every method on the 3 CRITERIA cost_effective, fewest_errors and alignment with stats.ranks, ties sharing their average rank, and sums the ranks. Cheapest and Most consistent design are bronze badges that never count toward it. Checked against awards.py at tdd-experiment c3dd988. verified

stats.py functions

Docstrings are quoted verbatim from stats.py at the snapshot sha. verified

stats.py functions
FunctionDocstring
wilson(lo, hi) Wilson score interval of k successes in n trials, which stays inside 0 to 1 at k = 0 and k = n.
fisher_exactTwo-sided Fisher exact p of the 2x2 table [[a, b], [c, d]]: the summed probability of every table with the same margins that is no more likely than this one. Integer weights keep ties exact.
holmHolm step-down adjusted p values in the order of ps, each capped at 1 and none below a smaller p's.
fisher_pairs{(i, j): (p, Holm p)} of the Fisher exact test of each pair of rows i < j of a k x 2 table, Holm-adjusted over every pair.
chi2_rowsPearson chi-square of a k x 2 table from each row's successes and size, 0 when a column is empty. An empty row adds nothing.
permutation_chi2(chi-square, p) of the k x 2 table of (successes, failures) rows. p is the share of seeded label shuffles, counting the observed one, whose chi-square reaches the observed.
deviationsEach value's absolute deviation from its group median.
brown_forsytheBrown-Forsythe F: one-way ANOVA F of each value's absolute deviation from its group median.
anova_fOne-way ANOVA F. inf when the values vary between groups only, 0 when they do not vary at all.
brown_forsythe_permutation(F, p) of brown_forsythe. p is the share of seeded shuffles of the pooled deviations from each value's own group median over groups of the same sizes, counting the observed one, whose F reaches the observed. Shuffling raw values would mix the group medians into the spread.
ranks1-based ranks with ties given their average rank.
mann_whitney_exact(U, p) of the exact two-sided Mann-Whitney test. U counts the pairs with x above y, ties as half. p is the share of every split of the pooled average ranks into a group of len(xs) whose rank sum lies at least as far from its mean as the observed, so ties stay exact. The C(n, len(xs)) splits suit small samples only.
weighted_kappaCohen's linearly weighted kappa of two raters' numeric ratings of the same items: 1 minus the mean |x - y| over its chance mean across every pairing of the two raters' ratings. None when chance disagreement is 0.
jaccardShared items over all items of two collections. Two empty ones agree fully and give 1.
spearmanSpearman rho as Pearson r of average ranks, None when either side has no spread.
spearman_testNo docstring in stats.py.
spearman_trial(rho, p, lo, hi, skipped): p is the share of seeded shuffles of ys, counting the observed one, whose |rho| reaches the observed, against the null rho = 0. lo to hi is the seeded percentile bootstrap 95% interval over resampled pairs, skipping the resamples with no spread, which skipped counts. lo and hi are None when no resample has spread. All None when either side has no spread.

Threats to validity

The report states its limits once, kept here word for word. The gaps after them come from the run records and the code docstrings in the snapshot.

Limits

What this study cannot show, stated once for the whole page.

Each survey method ran once on one task, so no significance test is possible and every survey gap is a single observation.

Tag: verified.

rep-1 and rep-2 each replicated only 4 methods, 5 times each, on one task with one model, with Classic greedy TDD in both. Their planned contrasts detect only large effects and the consistency comparison is exploratory.

Tag: verified.

full-1, rep-1 and rep-2 are three batches. The cost gap between full-1 and rep-1 is no harness change, since the prompts and launch scripts are identical. It coincides with an agent CLI change from 2.1.295 in full-1 to 2.1.308 in rep-1, checked on one agent transcript of each batch. rep-2 ran on CLI 2.1.309, as its contrast.json runtime records. Every agent in the costs.json files records CLI 2.1.295 in full-1 and 2.1.309 in rep-2, so full-1 and rep-2 differ by the same kind of CLI change.

Tag: verified.

Output tokens per request were 148 in full-1, 605 in rep-1 and 495 in rep-2, pooled over every clone of each run. The survey builds of classic, functions-first and noun-verb each cost less than all 5 of their rep-1 replicates, with median ratios of 1.13 to 1.45. With output tokens left out those ratios fall to 0.91 to 1.09 and only noun-verb's survey build still costs less than all 5 replicates. Cost comparisons of full-1 against rep-1 or rep-2 are not valid on full weights. The survey builds on the replicate figures are captioned as a different batch and no claim pools them. Consistency score C pools the raw costs of rep-1 and rep-2 across CLI builds 2.1.308 and 2.1.309. The drift test of Classic greedy TDD between those runs gives M p 0.310.

Tag: recomputed.

The rep-2 rating accepts none of the eight principles. Minimal Duplication, Modular Structure and Problem Alignment are tentative and Simple Constructs reaches alpha 0.52. So CQP accepted, which sums the three principles accepted on the full-1 finals, has no validity check on the rep-2 finals, and the rep-2 CQP sensitivity check is flagged.

Tag: recomputed.

One rep-2 high audit flag is a minor true positive. Most informative next test replicate rep-2-r09 ran ls ../ from its clone, which listed only the names in the clones dir, _heldout among them. It read no file outside its clone. The other 49 rep-2 high flags were judged false positives.

Tag: verified.

All 5 Held-out TDD replicates of rep-2 were rebuilt on 2026-10-30 at harness commit cc8c8cb, the held-out splitter merge, with the recorded harness delta of sha256 8fde5d73. The rest of the batch ended on 2026-10-29 at harness commit 809589d. So the Held-out TDD against Classic greedy TDD contrast spans two launches.

Tag: verified.

Whether the recorded token usage matches the real thinking volume of each request is unchecked.

Tag: unchecked.

All 11 methods reached the ceiling of 15/15, so the functional mark cannot rank them.

Tag: recomputed.

One model ran every agent, claude-opus-5-5 at medium effort.

Tag: verified.

Wall times were measured while all methods ran in parallel on one machine.

Tag: verified.

Bench times come from one machine and hold only for it.

Tag: verified.

Each rater saw a rebuilt clone whose history names no method and a prompt that names none. Comments inside src and test stay as written, so a comment could still hint at a method. The single review per method that the rating supersedes had no second reviewer.

Tag: verified.

The calibration gate was revised on 2026-10-26 after round-a failed. C5 now checks only problem_alignment on the starter stub (D20). C2 now passes a drop of 1 from a parent median of 3 (D19). The modular mutant was rebuilt as one god class (D21). The stub's round-b medians would fail the original C5, which needed all eight principles at 1 or 2.
