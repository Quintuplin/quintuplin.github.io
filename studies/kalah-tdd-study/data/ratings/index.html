Rating agreement (full-1)

Three blind raters scored each item from 1 to 5 on each cqp.html principle. Alpha is Krippendorff's ordinal alpha over the three rater slots, with a seeded bootstrap 95% interval in brackets. A resample whose values do not vary has no alpha and is skipped, so above 20% of the draws skipped an interval leans on the few varying items and is not estimable. An alpha's q is then not estimable too. q is the share of bootstrap alphas below 0.8 and below 0.7. The finals alpha sets each status: accepted at 0.8 or more, tentative at 0.7 or more and unchecked below. Only accepted and tentative principles join the headline. The finals set holds the 11 final clones. The all set holds every rated item of the published rounds, 20 in all. LOC and PMD rho are Spearman correlations of the finals medians with lines of code and with distinct PMD violations, with Holm-adjusted q. Tag: recomputed.
