Supporting data

The records behind the sections above, from the most processed to the raw. One row per method listed in run order. In the survey run full-1 each method ran once, so no significance test can separate two of its rows and a gap between two rows is a single observation. Run rep-1 built 4 methods 5 times each for the planned contrasts, which its own tables test. Run rep-2 built 4 methods 5 times each for the planned contrasts, which its own tables test.

The supporting data takes five pages.

  • Findings. The findings table and the audit flag dispositions.
  • Contrasts and replicates. Contrast, consistency and replicate tables for rep-1 and rep-2.
  • Method table. The method table, with each column linked to its measure.
  • Ratings. List reliability and rating agreement for each run.
  • Run records. The run records of full-1, rep-1 and rep-2.

Findings

All 11 methods score the functional mark 15/15, hidden 76/76, unseen 69/69 and acceptance 6/6. Only functions-first, mapped-path and noun-verb pass strict PMD compliance, as the reference does. The other 8 fail with 2 to 10 violations. The methods also differ in size, structure, mutation, cost, time, bench and rated code quality.

Finding: In the planned rep-1 contrast all 5 Anchor Engineering replicates reached adjusted mutation 1.000 against a median of 0.984 for Classic greedy TDD, exact Mann-Whitney U 25 and Holm p 0.016 over the two contrasts. Functions first, classes later and Noun-verb map did not differ, U 16 and Holm p 0.548. The CQP sensitivity check separated neither pair, Holm p 0.333.

Evidence: runs/rep-1/contrast.json contrasts and sensitivity, from harness/report/contrast.py.

result. Tag: recomputed.

Finding: In the planned rep-2 contrasts no survey leader differed from Classic greedy TDD on M, exact Mann-Whitney U 11 for Held-out TDD, 9 for Coarse-to-fine TDD and 17 for Most informative next test, each with Holm p 1.000 over the three contrasts. On adjusted mutation score, the fallback outcome, Held-out TDD scored lower than Classic greedy TDD, a median of 0.777 against 1.000, U 0 and Holm p 0.024. The other two leaders gave Holm p 1.000.

Evidence: runs/rep-2/contrast.json primary and secondary, from harness/report/contrast.py.

result. Tag: recomputed.

Finding: Over the seven replicated methods of rep-1 and rep-2 consistency score C runs from 0.38 for mutation-guided to 1.65 for noun-verb, and a permutation test of its spread across methods gives p 0.464. With 5 to 10 replicates each, no method is shown more consistent than another.

Evidence: replicate.consistency over runs/rep-1 and runs/rep-2 pooled at seed 20261024 with 10000 draws.

result. Tag: recomputed.

Finding: Three blind raters scored each final on the eight cqp.html principles. Their agreement over the 11 finals is accepted for Simple Constructs, Minimal Duplication and Modular Structure and tentative for Problem Alignment. The other four stay out of the headline. Every median of those four principles is 3, 4 or 5. Classic alone has median 3 on Simple Constructs. Modular Structure runs from 3 for annealed, classic and noun-verb to 5 for coarse-to-fine and held-out. Minimal Duplication is accepted on its alpha of 0.88, though its 95% interval runs from -0.03 to 1.00. Simple Constructs is accepted on its alpha of 1.00, which rests on that one varying item. Every rater gave classic 3 and every other final 4, so 34.7% of resamples hold no variation and its interval is not estimable. Alterability is not measured. The rating leaves it out and the single review that forecast it is superseded.

Evidence: rating.json of each final clone and runs/full-1/agreement.json, which agree.py recomputes from the three attempts per final.

Audit flag dispositions

Each row judges every high flag of one channel in one clone. The count comes from audit.json, which stays as computed.

Evidence: Both flags are held-out:s2 naming _heldout itself. One Bash call lists _heldout and _heldout/cases and one changes into _heldout to write cases with its scratch generator. Neither reads _heldout/hidden, and s2 may use _heldout/cases and _heldout/scratch.

Evidence: 3 flags mark held-out:s1's Write of test/kalah/PublicInterfaceTest.java and 4 mark its lines in commit 9640df5. The test reflects only on Kalah to check a public no-argument constructor and a public play(IO, boolean, boolean). It reads no spec and no MockIO state.
