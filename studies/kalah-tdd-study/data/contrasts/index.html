Consistency table

Exploratory, since only the replicated methods enter it. It pools every replicate of runs rep-1 and rep-2 per method. Each cell is a relative spread, the method's mean absolute deviation from its own median over the mean deviation of the methods off the metric's bound. Lower is more consistent and 1 is the average. At bound means at least 60 percent of builds sit at the metric's bound. Not comparable means fewer than two methods sit off it. C is the mean over unadjusted mutation, classes and cost to completion. The tests are seeded permutation tests with 10,000 draws that shuffle each build's deviations from its own method's median. Tag: recomputed.
