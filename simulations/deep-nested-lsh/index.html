Deep Nested LSH Explorer

Classical LSH bands signature rows with AND inside each band and OR across bands to sharpen the line between pairs judged similar and pairs judged dissimilar. Deep Nested LSH adds further alternating AND and OR levels and misclassifies fewer pairs than shallow banding at a matched signature length.

Terms used on this page
LSH
Locality sensitive hashing. It hashes documents so similar pairs likely collide and become candidates without comparing every pair.
The study
A search over trees by depth and row budget, scored on one generated corpus of 1000 documents. Most of its documents form clusters of near duplicates. The presets and their trees come from this search.
Jaccard similarity s
Shared shingles of two documents over all their shingles. A shingle here is a run of three consecutive words after lowercasing.
MinHash row
One hash value per document. Two documents agree on a row with probability s.
Signature length n
The number of MinHash rows a tree reads. It is the product of the arities.
Row budget
The largest n a tree may use. The presets compare trees within 1000 or 10000 rows.
Tuple d and depth k
d = (d0, d1, …) lists one arity per level. Depth k is the number of levels. n is the product of the arities.
Band
d0 rows joined by AND. It fires when the two documents agree on every one of its rows.
Arity dj
How many nodes of level j − 1 each node at level j combines. Level 0 combines rows.
AND level and OR level
An AND level fires only when every child fires. An OR level fires when any child fires.
Cascade reading
Odd levels OR their children and even levels AND them. Depth 2 is standard (r, b) banding.
(r, b) banding
Standard LSH with b bands of r rows each. As a tuple it is (r, b) with r = d0 and b = d1.
Flat reading
Every level above the bands ORs. It collapses to two-level banding (d0, n / d0).
Root and rung
The ladder holds one rung per level with the signature rows at the bottom and the root on top. Each rung sets its level's arity and shows the children of one node. The root is the single top node.
Rung labels Lj
L0 is the level of bands, L1 the level above it and so on up to the root. The AND or OR tag beside each label names that level's operation.
Hidden tile +k · j on
A rung draws only its first children. The last tile counts the k children not drawn and, after a run, the j of them that fired.
Candidate
A pair whose root fires. Only candidates go on to an exact check.
S curve P(s)
The candidate probability curve. P(s) is the chance that a pair of similarity s becomes a candidate.
Per-level curves
Each faint curve traces one level over s. It is the curve the subtree up to that level follows. Hover or focus a rung to pick out its curve.
p gauges
The p value and bar on each rung give the chance that a node at that level fires at the current s. The rows rung shows s itself.
Target t
The Jaccard value meant to split pairs. Pairs at or above t should become candidates.
Ideal step
The perfect filter at t. It is 0 below t and 1 at or above t.
Threshold t̂
The s where the candidate probability P(s) crosses one half. The study solves d1 so t̂ lies within 0.01 of t.
Solve d1 for t
Solves one arity for the target t. At depth 1 it sets d0 to the value with the closest t̂. At depth 2 or more it solves d1 for t and tries the whole numbers on either side of that solution. It sets d1 to the sharpest of them whose t̂ lies within 0.01 of t, or to the one with the closer t̂ when neither does. When the solution falls below 1.5 or does not exist it leaves d1 unchanged. A tree whose t̂ lands more than 0.01 from t is flagged as unmatched.
Width W
The span of s over which P(s) climbs from 0.05 to 0.95. Smaller is sharper.
Sharpest
The smallest width W among the trees whose t̂ lies within 0.01 of t. The search tries every d0, solves d1 for t and tries every arity up to the free-arity cap at the levels above d1.
Free-arity cap 24
The largest arity the study's search tries at levels 2 and up. d0 and d1 have no cap.
Sharpest at depth k
Swaps in the sharpest tree of the current depth whose rows fit within the current n at t. It covers trees of at most 10 million rows.
Depth cap
The explorer stops at the deepest depth the study's search covers.
Error area E
The area between P(s) and the ideal step at t over s from 0 to 1. The FP area lies left of t and the FN area right of it. It is a theory value.
Expected counts (theory)
P(s) applied to every pair of the study corpus. E[FP] sums P(s) over the pairs below t. E[FN] sums 1 − P(s) over the pairs at or above t. E[errors] adds the two after rounding each.
Measured mean ± sd
The errors the study counted with real MinHash signatures over several random draws of the hashes h(x) = (a x + c) mod 261 − 1. The readout gives their mean and sample standard deviation when the study ran that tree at that t.
Sharpest banding within n rows
The sharpest depth 2 banding whose rows fit within the tree's n. The readout compares the tree with it. The page skips that search for trees above 10 million rows.
Quorum check
Compares a pair on all n rows and keeps it when at least ⌈t n⌉ rows agree. Its curve is the binomial tail of n rows. The readout gives its expected errors and the errors the study measured when it checked every pair this way.
Retrieval banding (d0, f−) and kept bands
The kept bands are the bands that take the first child at every AND level on the way to the root. There are f− of them with d0 rows each. A pair is retrieved when any kept band fires. The readout gives the expected number of retrieved pairs.
Pipeline
Retrieves with the kept bands and then applies the quorum check to the retrieved pairs. Its curve is Q(s) − (1 − sd0)f− P[A ≥ ⌈t n⌉]. Q(s) is the quorum check curve and A counts the agreeing rows of a pair whose kept bands all miss. With the pipeline on each trial follows it and the root reads accepted or rejected. The page computes it for trees of at most 20000 rows.
TP and TN
A true positive is a pair at or above t that becomes a candidate. A true negative is a pair below t that stays out.
FP and FN
A false positive is a pair below t that becomes a candidate. A false negative is a pair at or above t that does not.
f+ and f−
Tail exponents. f+ is the product of the AND arities with d0. f− is the product of the OR arities. f+ f− = n.
Measured slope
The local slope of a log-log tail at its far end, at s 10−6 for the FP tail and at 1 − s 10−6 for the FN tail. It approaches f+ and f−. The tail cards and the Measured slope captions show it.
Trial pair and realised s′
Two random sets of shingle ids at the corpus median set size, drawn from the corpus vocabulary. Whole ids allow only some Jaccard values, so a pair asked for at s realises the nearest one, s′. Each row hashes the pair as the study does, with h(x) = (a x + c) mod 261 − 1, and agrees when the least hash of the two sets falls on a shared id.
Trial dots and Wilson interval
Run sends one random pair at s up the tree. 1000 at s and Sweep send 1000 random pairs at each s and plot a dot at s′ for the share that became candidates. Its bar is the 95% Wilson score interval for that share. The dots should sit on the curve.
Trial seed
Seeds the row hashes and the random pairs. Clear or a new seed drops the trial dots and restarts both from the shown seed, so the same seed replays the same trials. It affects only the random trials.
At its own threshold
The preset sets t to the tree's own t̂.
Standard banding at 10000 rows
The study's best depth 2 (r, b) banding within 10000 rows at each target.
Sharpest tree per depth at 10000 rows
The sharpest tree at each depth the study searched within 10000 rows at each target.
Equal-rows ladder
The sharpest tree at each depth whose rows lie within 5% of the rows the best banding uses at that target. It compares depths at a matched signature length.
Least error area beyond depth k
Trees from the error area search deeper than the sharpest tree presets. They show only when they fit within the depth cap. Each label names the search kind and the depth.
two-tier, max-depth and all-2s
Kinds of error area search. Two-tier chooses the depth and the arities together for the least error area. Max-depth takes the deepest depth at which a matched tree fits. All-2s sets every arity to 2 with one 3 where it fits and has no threshold match.
Arity families at t 0.5
Trees at depth 3 and up within 10000 rows matched to t 0.5, searched with arities up to 60 above d1. The families differ in where the large arities sit.
head-heavy, even-weights and tail-heavy
Head-heavy puts the large arities at the low levels near the rows. Tail-heavy puts them at the high levels near the root. Even-weights keeps the arities as close to equal as the threshold allows.
Flat reading collapse (7, 23, 3) and (7, 69)
Under the flat reading (7, 23, 3) collapses to the banding (7, 69). Switch on Flat reading to compare the two trees.
